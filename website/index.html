Only what you put in.

Burrow is a calm music and video player for young children. You fill it with your own songs, videos, Apple Music and YouTube links; your child sees big, quiet tiles and nothing else.

Download on the App Store

iPhone and iPad · iOS 17 or later · English, Nederlands, Deutsch, Français, Español

Built to stop, not to keep them watching

Most apps for children are made to hold attention for as long as possible. Burrow has no feed, no search, no recommendations and no autoplay into something you have never seen. When the playlist ends, it ends. When the daily time is up, it stops.

How it works

Make a tile

One tile is one playlist, music or video, with a picture your child recognises.

Fill it

Add audio and video files from your device, songs from Apple Music, or YouTube links.

Hand it over

Your child taps a tile and it plays. Everything you set up sits behind a grown-ups question.

What is in it

Works offline

Your own files play anywhere: in the car, on a plane, in a tent. No Wi-Fi needed.

A profile per child

Each child gets their own tiles, colours and animal. Share a tile between siblings in one tap.

Screen time per child

Set a daily limit. When it is reached, playback stops and Burrow goes back to the profile picker.

Six calm games

Matching Pairs, Odd One Out, Copy the Pattern, Tic Tac Toe, Four in a Row and Hangman without the gallows. No scores, no streaks.

Lock the screen

Lock a playing video against little hands. Unlocking takes a three-second press a toddler will not find.

Ready-made playlists

Open a playlist someone shared as a link, or start from our page of ready-made playlists.

Your data stays yours

No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Profiles and tiles sync through your own private iCloud. Nothing is sent to us.

Backups you hold

Export everything, your own files included, into one file and read it back on a new device.
