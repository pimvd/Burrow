Burrow — Playlists

Ready-made playlists for Burrow. Open this page on the iPhone or iPad that has Burrow and tap Add to Burrow. Burrow asks for the grown-up's year of birth, shows you the playlist, and adds it as a tile only once you choose a profile and tap Add tile.

The free version adds the first three songs of a playlist; the unlock adds them all. Every song plays from YouTube, so it needs an internet connection and keeps working only for as long as YouTube allows it.
