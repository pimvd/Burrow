Burrow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Burrow has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, or receive any personal data about you or your child. There is no server of ours for anything to be sent to.

Where your content lives

Profiles, tiles, playlists, settings, and usage statistics are stored on your device and synced through your own private iCloud so your family's devices stay in step. That data sits in your personal Apple account, not ours — we have no access to it and no way to read it. Nothing is shared between different Apple accounts.

The audio, video, and pictures you add yourself are copied into the app's own storage on the device. They are not synced to iCloud by the app; they travel only in your iPhone's own iCloud backup, or in a backup file you export deliberately.

A backup file you export is an ordinary zip on your device. What happens to it afterwards is entirely up to you.

Services outside Burrow

Two kinds of playlist item are played by someone else, and it is worth being clear about what that means.

Apple Music. If you add an Apple Music song, it plays through Apple Music using your own subscription, and Apple's privacy policy applies to that playback. Burrow asks for Apple Music permission only when you add such an item.

YouTube. If you paste a YouTube link, the video is played by YouTube's own embedded player inside the app. That means Google receives the requests any YouTube playback involves. Burrow shows the video without recommendations, comments, or any way for a child to browse onward, but it cannot prevent Google from seeing that the video was played. If you would rather no third party is involved at all, use audio and video files you add yourself.

Your child

There is nothing in the child's part of the app that collects anything. No text entry, no messaging, no sharing, no sign-in, and no links out to the web. Usage statistics — which tiles were opened and for how long — are recorded so a parent can see them in the app, and they stay in your own storage like everything else. You can delete them at any time in the parent area.

Purchase

The one-time Burrow unlock is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers that transaction.

Notifications

If you turn on the monthly backup reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved and no notification is sent from us.
