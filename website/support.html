Burrow — Support

Burrow is a small, quiet place for a young child: music, video, and calm games, holding only what you put there. No recommendations, no autoplay into the unknown, no ads.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add something for my child?
Tap the grown-ups button, answer the year-of-birth question, then go to Tiles. A tile is one playlist — music or video — and you fill it with audio and video files from your device, Apple Music songs, or YouTube links.

Can two children share a tile?
Yes. Open Profiles, tap the child who should also see it, and switch it on under Shared tiles. It stays one tile with one playlist, so editing it changes it for both.

My child keeps pausing the video by touching the screen.
While a video is playing, tap the padlock in the top corner of the player. Burrow then ignores every touch: nothing pauses, skips, or closes, and the picture itself stops answering taps too. To undo it, press and hold that same padlock for three seconds — a ring fills up as you hold, and there is no code to type, so you can do it with the device still in your child's hands. It stays locked until you unlock it, even once the video has ended.

Why does a YouTube item in a music tile play no picture?
That is deliberate: in a music tile you get the sound only. Its sound also stops when Burrow is minimised, because iOS suspends the player it uses. The rest of the playlist keeps going.

Music stops when I leave the app.
Open Profiles, tap the child, and turn on Keep playing on the lock screen. Music carries on; video always stops, on purpose.

New phone — do I have to buy it again?
No. The unlock is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account. Sign in with the same account and your profiles, tiles, and playlists come back through iCloud on their own. If the unlock does not appear, use Restore purchase on the unlock screen.

My tiles came back but the songs and videos are missing.
iCloud syncs the profiles, tiles, and playlists, but not the audio, video, and pictures you added yourself — those are only on the device you added them to. Bring them across with a backup: on the old phone use Export and import → Create backup, then read that file on the new one.

What does the free version include?
One child profile, five tiles per profile, three items per playlist, and two games. The one-time unlock lifts all of that: unlimited profiles and tiles, up to 50 items per playlist, and every game.

Will YouTube keep working?
It works today, and we intend to keep it working. But it is the one part of Burrow that depends on somebody else's permission. YouTube videos play through Google's own embedded player, and Burrow can offer them only for as long as YouTube allows it — that is Google's decision, not ours, and it can change without warning. If it does, YouTube items will stop playing and no update of ours can bring them back. Everything else is unaffected: audio and video files you added from your own device, Apple Music, the games and the tiles themselves all keep working. If you want a playlist you can rely on for years, build it from your own files.

What exactly does the unlock buy?
It lifts Burrow's own limits — profiles, tiles, playlist length and games. It is not a payment for YouTube, Apple Music, or any content; those belong to Google and Apple and follow their own rules and availability.

Can I try the full version first?
Yes. On the unlock screen, tap Try everything free for 14 days. The App Store asks you to confirm, as with any purchase, but the trial costs nothing and there is nothing to cancel — after a fortnight Burrow goes back to the free version, and everything you made stays exactly where it is. The trial can be started once per Apple Account.

How do I limit how long my child can use it?
In Profiles, per child, set a daily limit. When the time is up, playback stops and Burrow returns to the profile picker.

How do I change the language?
Burrow follows your system language: English, German, Spanish, French, and Dutch.
